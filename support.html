Support

Help with Vanylla, the photo and video editor for iPhone. Find an answer below, or get in touch.

Contact support Email us

Or write to vanylla.app@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, its iOS version and what you were doing. To include Vanylla’s diagnostics, send them by email: in Vanylla, Settings, then Export diagnostics.

Vanylla Pro and purchases

Vanylla is free to download. Vanylla Pro is one purchase that unlocks every tool and look.

How do I restore Vanylla Pro on a new iPhone, or after reinstalling?

Usually there is nothing to do: Pro comes back by itself on any iPhone signed in to the Apple Account you bought it with.

If it doesn’t, open Vanylla and tap Settings (the cog at the top of the Library), then Vanylla Pro › Restore purchase. Every Pro screen also has Restore. Restoring needs an internet connection.

Is Vanylla Pro a subscription?

No. One purchase. No subscription. You pay once, and Pro stays unlocked.

Vanylla says “Waiting for approval”

Ask to Buy is on for your Apple Account: Vanylla Pro unlocks when the person who approves your purchases says yes.

Does Vanylla work offline?

Yes. Editing works with no connection, and so does Pro once it is unlocked. Buying, restoring and seeing the price need the App Store, so they wait until you are back online.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com. After a refund, nothing you made is deleted: your edits stay in your library, and saving or exporting an edit that uses Pro tools asks for Pro again.

What is free, and what needs Pro?

Free: light and color with Auto; crop, straighten and perspective; the Chrome filter pack; Recipes; collages; your whole library; and export of edits made with the free tools, with no watermark.

Pro: the full filter library, every film effect, curves, HSL and color wheels, denoise and sharpen, Repair and Focus, and pasting edits or applying Recipes to many photos at once.

Photos access

Vanylla copies the photos and videos you choose into its own library on your iPhone, and edits those copies.

Why does Vanylla ask for access to Photos?

To import. Vanylla asks the first time you import, after a screen that explains why, and never when it opens. You can allow all your photos, or only the ones you pick.

Your first Save to Photos asks again, so Vanylla can keep your exports together in a “Vanylla” album. If you say no, it asks only to add photos, and saves them without the album.

Can I use Vanylla without giving it access?

Yes. On the import screen, tap Choose photos one at a time. Apple’s photo picker opens, and needs no permission.

How do I change what Vanylla can see?

Open the Settings app, then Apps › Vanylla › Photos, and choose Full Access, Limited Access or None. Vanylla’s own Settings › Privacy shows what it can see now and opens that page for you.

A photo takes a while to import, or needs a connection

If you use iCloud Photos, a photo or video that is only in iCloud is downloaded when you import it. That needs an internet connection; the copy in Vanylla then stays on your iPhone.

Why won’t a video import?

Vanylla edits H.264 and HEVC videos up to 4K (4096 × 2160), up to 60 frames per second and up to 10 minutes long. An HDR video must be 10-bit. When a video can’t be imported, Vanylla says which of these it is.

Storage and your library

Vanylla has no account and no cloud of its own: your library lives on your iPhone.

How much space does Vanylla use?

In Vanylla, open Settings › Storage. It shows your originals, your edits and library data, and the caches.

Free up space clears the caches, which Vanylla makes again when it needs them. It never removes your photos, videos or edits.

How do I free more space?

Delete what you no longer need: in the Library, select the items and tap Delete. Vanylla asks first, then deletes them for good: there is no Trash. The photos in your Photos library are not touched.

Is my Vanylla library backed up?

Yes, as part of your iPhone’s own backup. Vanylla keeps a copy of every photo and video you import, and your edits and Recipes, in its library on your iPhone, and that library is included when your iPhone backs up to iCloud or to your computer. Restoring that backup brings your library back.

It also means your Vanylla library counts toward your iCloud storage. If your backup no longer fits, delete in Vanylla what you no longer need.

How do I move my library to a new iPhone?

Set up the new iPhone from a backup of the old one, or with Quick Start: your Vanylla library comes with it. Your Pro purchase comes back with your Apple Account.

Exporting

Exports keep the full size unless you choose a smaller one, and never carry a watermark.

How do I save an edit to Photos, or share it?

In the editor, tap Save, then Save to Photos or Share. In the Library, select one or more items and tap Export or Share.

Photos save as HEIF, JPEG or PNG; videos as HEVC (.mov) or H.264 (.mp4). Set your usual choices in Settings › Export defaults.

Why does exporting ask for Pro?

Export is free, with no watermark, for edits made with the free tools. When an edit uses a Pro tool or look (a Pro filter, film effects, curves, HSL, color wheels, denoise and sharpen, Repair or Focus), Vanylla asks for Pro before it exports it.

You can still try every Pro tool: your edits stay in your library either way.

Vanylla says there isn’t enough space

A full-size export needs room on your iPhone while it is made. Free some space (the Settings app, General › iPhone Storage, or Settings › Storage › Free up space in Vanylla), then try again.

Does an exported photo include its location?

Not unless you want it to. Location is left out until you turn on Settings › Export defaults › Include location. Camera details (such as the lens and exposure) are kept unless you turn off Include camera info.

Updates

Your Vanylla 1.x unlock does not expire. Every 1.x update we release is included at no cost, and we will keep releasing 1.x updates for at least two years from launch.

The app has no subscription and its local editing features do not depend on our servers.

Privacy

Editing happens on your iPhone. Vanylla never uploads your photos, videos, or audio to our servers. It has no account, no ads and no tracking. The Privacy Policy has the details.

What is in Export diagnostics?

A small file to help us find a problem: counts, sizes, your settings, error codes and recent crash reports. It holds no photos, videos, audio or locations, and no names of your files or Recipes. It leaves your iPhone only if you send it.
