Custom Recipes

Your look, saved as a Recipe.

A Recipe remembers every setting of an edit: light, color, filter and film. Think of it as a preset made from your own photo, ready for the next one.

  • Save any editEvery slider, filter and film setting, under a name you choose.
  • Apply to one photo, or manyGive a whole set the same look in one go, with a strength slider to taste.
  • Share itSend a Recipe to a friend and they can use your look too.
